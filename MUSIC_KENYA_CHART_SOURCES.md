# Kenya music chart sources

Research checked on 4 November 2026. The curated starter tracks in Music Studio link to YouTube uploads and are embedded through YouTube's own player; no audio files are copied into Mukuyu.

## Current chart snapshot

Soundcharts' Kenya YouTube weekly chart for the week of 2 November 2026 ranked “I'll Be Okay” by Bien at #1 (2.3M views), “Kitemeo” by Kapitani at #4 (672K), “Shing” by Kapitani & Toxic Lyrikali at #5 (669K), and “Kwa Neema” by Otile Brown & Darassa at #11 (451K). Kworb's Kenya YouTube trending list also showed “I'll Be Okay” at #1, “Kwa Neema” at #4, “Shing” at #7 and “Mafia Boss” by Toxic Lyrikali at #10 in its 3 November snapshot. The playlist starter uses the four chart-linked videos that had retrievable official YouTube metadata at implementation time.

Apple Music's official “Top 100: Kenya” page describes itself as the most-played songs in Kenya and says it updates every day. Mukuyu's quick-add option links to that live page as an official provider playlist, so the chart can change without a code update. The provider still controls playback and regional/account availability.
